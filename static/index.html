Vivre

Most accidents aren't surprises, they're unwatched. Tell Vivre what to look for, in a sentence, and it keeps watch.

One sentence per line, up to 3

A prototype, not a safety device. It complements smoke alarms and medical-alert gear. It checks every few seconds and can miss things.

Frames are sent to Meta's API for analysis. Vivre keeps the last few in memory during a session and never saves them. The microphone only turns on for spoken rules and rules about sounds, and those few seconds of sound are treated the same way. Alert clips stay in this browser until you close the tab.

Camera and video options

How Vivre works

You describe what to watch for, in a sentence. Muse Spark turns it into a rule you can read on the card. Every few seconds, Meta's models check the camera, and for rules about speech, the sound. Plain code keeps the time. For sounds like clapping, a recognizer in your browser listens instead.

When a rule fires, you get a chime, a spoken alert and a short clip to check. Muse Wonder explains what happened, or double-checks first for camera rules.
