Vivre

Most accidents aren't surprises, they're unwatched. Tell Vivre what to look for, in a sentence, and it keeps watch.

A prototype, not a safety device. It complements smoke alarms and medical-alert gear. It checks every few seconds and can miss things.

Frames are sent to Meta's API for analysis. Vivre keeps the last few in memory during a session and never saves them. The microphone only turns on for spoken rules and rules about sounds, and those few seconds of sound are treated the same way.
